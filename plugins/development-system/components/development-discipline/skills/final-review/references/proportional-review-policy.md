# Proportional independent review policy

## Default and evidence boundary

Protocol v3 requires one complete risk-selected independent review round by
default. Every applicable responsibility receives a genuine fresh-context
reviewer. Review coverage, resolved obligations, and exact-revision evidence
control completion; legacy clean-streak counters do not certify v3 readiness.

The same connected `workspace-reader.status` response must attest protocol
version at least 3, minimum one, scoped coverage, explicit policy migration,
and durable pending-assignment recovery. Missing or mismatched capability means
zero enforced credit and no terminal readiness claim. Update version-matched
plugin/binaries, restart the harness, and repeat that exact probe. Manual prose
cannot replace the coordinator or authorize external actions.

## Select responsibility and effort

The independent risk scout evaluates every supplied dimension against the
change, acceptance criteria, intended deployment, actual trust boundaries,
consumers, and dependencies. Select all applicable or uncertain dimensions,
including correctness. A low overall rating cannot hide an applicable security
or other dimension. Map every changed path to meaningful review coverage.

Declare `coverage_policy.artifact_kind` and per-requirement overrides where a
change mixes code, tooling, models/documents, instructions, and rendered
artifacts. Model/document review checks semantic completeness, consistency,
requirements, coverage, and communication. Production-code review checks
executable behavior and deployment obligations. A model that describes future
production behavior does not thereby require production-code RED tests or
scale review. Instructions/tooling still receive security scrutiny where they
change authority, untrusted-input handling, protected data, or side effects.

`production-risk-footguns` is selected for concrete production-scale,
resource-use, data-access, unsafe-default, burst, or deployment consequences.
Its applicability is not established by a filename or proximity to code.

Each requirement normally uses `required_samples: 1`. The independent assessor
may select 2–10 samples only for high or exceptional consequential residual
risk, recording the consequence, unresolved uncertainty, affected scope, and
why that count improves scrutiny. Do not multiply unaffected lenses. Time,
budget, reviewer preference, or a stuck task never justifies either escalation
or a waiver. Model capability and reasoning effort remain task-local routing
choices rather than substitutes for review evidence.

Ordinary delta reassessment preserves every previously selected responsibility
and its recorded sample floor. Reject omitted responsibilities or lower counts;
a repair, budget decision, or changed model-produced policy cannot silently
remove outstanding obligations. Proven unchanged receipts satisfy those floors
without new reviewer runs.

Exceptional dimensions require at least two samples and supported trigger
evidence. Preserve these exact trigger IDs:

- `destructive-or-irreversible-operation`
- `authentication-or-authorization-boundary`
- `sensitive-data-migration`
- `cryptographic-behavior`
- `safety-critical-behavior`

An exceptional overall profile needs both a supported trigger and an explicitly
exceptional dimension. Concrete mitigations may keep a supported boundary below
exceptional; retain its evidence rather than automatically escalating peers.

## Receipts, repairs, and invalidation

Every credited result binds the assignment, actual reviewed scope, immutable
source snapshot, shared verification evidence, assigned model role, fresh
context, and actual post-result closure. The caller appends lifecycle
attestations only after observing the real reviewer lifecycle. Never invent a
model, closure, inspection, verifier, CI result, or CodeRabbit result.

`coverage_evidence.dependency_blobs` includes every assigned scope path and
actually checked dependency as raw Git mode:blob identity, or `absent` for a
reviewed deletion. The host observes bytes, modes, and safe-path absence
independently; omitted observation is never proof of deletion. Each receipt keeps its original revision, reviewer,
and evidence identity. Current applicability proofs reference it; they do not
rewrite history or claim a fresh run.

For a repair, require fresh meaningful regression evidence and independent
review of affected behavior, callers, contracts, consumers, and dependencies.
The independent delta scout supplies the modern policy, affected dimensions,
`whole_scope_affected`, and a concrete `invalidation_rationale` explaining
source, behavior, scope, configuration, environment, inputs, and freshness.
The host re-observes retained dependencies; changed or missing identities
invalidate their dependent coverage even if the caller declares it unaffected.
A new or expanded responsibility requires fresh coverage.

Preserve unaffected receipts only when the original claim and current
applicability are proven. A missing observation never proves deletion or
unchanged behavior. Changed dependency modes, renames, deleted paths,
configuration, inputs, environment, or freshness invalidate affected evidence.
Unproven historical coverage is unavailable rather than guessed.

Reset the whole scope only for independently evidenced cross-cutting impact or
shared provenance whose affected scope cannot be isolated. Record the reason
and retain history/blockers. Metadata-only commits, signatures, staging
partitions, and coordinator bookkeeping do not reset identical source review;
required exact-identity and delivery checks still run.

Rejected findings and properly dispositioned report-only feedback stay visible
without a ritual finding-free rerun. Confirmed acceptance, correctness,
security, safety, verification, or delivery blockers remain required work.
Caller defense alone cannot close a disputed obligation. Use genuine
independent adjudication with frozen targets and provenance.

Malformed, missing, stale, reused, or unattested evidence earns zero credit.
Replace its assignment and preserve independently valid peers. A malformed
verifier renews adjudication over the frozen findings, not source discovery.
Unsupported repeats of a rejected finding require new contradictory evidence,
relevant source change, or a demonstrated verification gap before reopening.

## Rendered behavior and model semantics

Visual review follows changed rendered behavior, not every transitive source
hash. A source change first receives impact assessment. Retaining a separately
scoped `rendered-artifact` judgment requires `render_continuity`, bound to the
current diff and exact current shared-test `artifact_reference`, covering all
assigned artifact paths and rendering/freshness conditions. The proof is either
`unchanged-render-inputs` or `current-output-equivalence`; an independent scout
must actually inspect the referenced evidence. Saved image hashes alone do not
prove current-source rendering or unchanged coverage.

Reinspect changed, new, or unproven rendered behavior. Keep prior source and
visual approvals intact and add the current continuity proof. Canonical model
or requirement changes require semantic review even when images are identical;
visual continuity is not semantic approval or human business acceptance.
Do not add a renderer, baseline bypass, or Foundry-specific integration here.

## Recovery, legacy compatibility, and delivery

Existing v2 records and legacy clients without `coverage_policy` keep their
recorded three complete clean rounds and full-reset rules. Do not reinterpret
old counters, remove requirements from serialized state, or lower a gate to
recover failure. Modern scout schemas explicitly require `coverage_policy`.

`final_review.migrate_policy` first previews a source-bound fresh independent
risk assignment while the old policy remains authoritative. Close or truthfully
attest never-started prior assignments, then resubmit the same operation with
the genuine assessment and `prior_assignment_closures`. Migration is durable
and idempotent. Preserve baseline, source, history, unresolved findings,
resolutions, and failure holds. Missing legacy dependency/freshness evidence
cannot become narrower reusable v3 credit; historical evidence remains
inspectable. Changed source requires its separate delta workflow.

Use `pending_assignments` and exact idempotent retries after interruption.
A 75-minute assessment or iteration limit does not waive obligations.
Strong readiness analysis consumes complete coverage plus exact final-revision
verification, actual required CI/CodeRabbit evidence, signatures, and authorized
delivery. Queued/running/older-SHA CI is insufficient; failed required CI remains
blocking until truthful causal recovery reaches terminal success.

Tiber's optional configured minimum can be one or two; stronger explicit
settings remain enforced. Keep committed policy, reviewer independence, exact
fingerprints, current evidence, and completion/trailer gates. Record actual
whole-scope review completion, not one task-board record per lens or fabricated
repetitions of the same receipt. Changing a stronger repository requirement
needs an explicit reviewed configuration change, not a runtime bypass.

Yield reports distinguish complete rounds, scoped repairs, additional risk
samples, assignment replacement, verifier replacement, and retained evidence.
Reused source results are not fresh reviewers. Legacy or pruned counts remain
unavailable where evidence is missing.
